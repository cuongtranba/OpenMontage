## Purpose

Local FastAPI-style server plus browser board opened via `python -m backlot open <project-id>`: discovers projects by their project.json marker, watches checkpoints and artifacts, renders the stage rail, script, scene filmstrip, decision log, and cost snapshot live. Derives everything from disk — holds no state of its own and never blocks a production run. Not responsible for approving gates; approval happens in the agent conversation and is persisted by c3-103.
